HOW IT WORKS

01 · TRIAGE

Catch the lie

Our Round 1 model (LR + gradient boosting + random forest) scores every log and explains why: wrong module named, fake quoted value, redacted or out-of-range reading, calm text on a CRIT.

02 · EMAIL SPECIALIST

Wake the right crew

Each true fault is matched to the specialist for its module and the least-busy one gets an alert with the evidence, similar past incidents and the booked slot.

03 · VECTOR MEMORY

Never forget

Every incident is embedded into a local Chroma store, so the crew can search "what happened like this before?" and each alert carries its nearest neighbours.

04 · FIX BOOKER

Schedule the repair

Highest-risk faults first. Appends log_id, crew_id, start, end, job to schedule.csv with zero double-bookings.
